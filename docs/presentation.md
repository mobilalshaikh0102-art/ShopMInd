### Slide 2: The Problem
**Running an online business doesn't scale linearly with human effort.**
- **Fragmented Data:** Merchants jump between Shopify, Stripe, Shiprocket, and spreadsheets.
- **Manual Overhead:** Writing product descriptions, answering basic support tickets, and tracking inventory eats up 80% of an operator's day.
- **Reactive, not Proactive:** Restocks happen *after* stock runs out. Angry customers are identified *after* they leave a bad review.

---

### Slide 3: The Solution
**An Intelligent, Agent-Driven Back Office.**
ShopMind isn't just a dashboard; it's a team of autonomous AI agents working 24/7.
- **Unified Hub:** Deep integrations with Shopify, Stripe, and Logistics.
- **AI Automation:** From SEO copywriting to dynamic demand forecasting.
- **Human-in-the-Loop:** AI makes suggestions (e.g., "Order 500 units from Vendor X"), but humans hold the keys to approve high-risk financial actions.

### Slide 4: Key Features (Live Demo Focus)
**What ShopMind can do right now:**
1. **Dynamic Dashboard:** Role-based access (Admin, Ops, Support, Analyst).
2. **AI Support Copilot:** Instantly analyze customer sentiment in tickets and draft responses.
3. **Inventory & Logistics:** Barcode scanning and automated Purchase Order generation.
4. **Customer CRM:** RFM scoring to instantly identify VIPs vs. Churn-risk customers.
5. **Agent Executions Log:** Full transparency into the "brain" of the AI.
